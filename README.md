# VAT Invoice Generator (Wholesale & Retail)

A modern, high-precision Irish VAT Invoice application designed for **Get Connected (Wholesale)** and **I Digital Fun (Retail)**.

## 🚀 Features

- **Dual Invoicing Modes**:
  - **Wholesale (Get Connected)**: Professional wholesale invoicing with custom banner, itemized product tables, tax rate customization, and total due calculations matching the official Get Connected template.
  - **Retail (I Digital Fun)**: Branch-specific retail POS invoicing with 13 official store locations from the master directory, gross-to-net automatic pricing (`Price / 1.23`), and VAT itemization.
- **Store Directory Integration**:
  - Preloaded with all 13 official store locations (Portlaoise, Tullamore, Mullingar, Bridgewater Arklow, Thurles, Limerick, Newbridge, Maynooth, Letterkenny, Killarney, Tralee, and Get Connected branches).
- **Pixel-Perfect A4 Print & PDF**:
  - Clean `@media print` styling optimized for single-page A4 printing and direct PDF downloads.
- **Dynamic Catalog & Quick Presets**:
  - 1-click addition of top wholesale accessories, retail screen protectors, cables, chargers, and devices (iPhones, Samsungs, iPads).
- **Persistence & Export**:
  - Auto-save drafts and local invoice history manager.
  - 1-click JSON backup & import.
  - 1-click CSV export for accounting and Excel workflows.

## 📦 Deployment

Configured for static hosting on **Cloudflare Pages** and **GitHub Pages**.
